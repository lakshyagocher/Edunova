Our Story

About EduNova

Built by a student. Grown by thousands. Loved by India.

Our Mission

Making Quality Education Accessible to All

EduNova was born out of a simple frustration — finding quality, concise, and affordable academic resources for Indian students was needlessly hard.

Our founder, a Class 12 student from Jaipur, started by uploading handwritten notes online for friends. When thousands of students started using them, EduNova was born. Today, we're a community-powered education platform helping students across India learn better, ask smarter questions, and achieve extraordinary results.

We believe that every student — regardless of their school, city, or economic background — deserves access to the best possible learning resources.
